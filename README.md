# Crumb Club 🍞

A playful campus food rescue prototype for last-call snacks. Neighbors can find surplus food, reserve portions, and collect them during a local pickup window.

## Try the prototype

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or server is required.

## What you can try

- Browse sample snack drops and search or filter by dietary tag, free offers, and offers ending soon.
- Reserve portions, view pickup codes, or cancel a reservation.
- Use the Partner Board to post a drop, manage portions, and verify a pickup code.
- Reset the demo to restore its sample data.

The demo stores its sample offers and changes in your browser's local storage. It has no backend, accounts, payments, or delivery. Ask the food partner directly about ingredients and allergens before pickup.
